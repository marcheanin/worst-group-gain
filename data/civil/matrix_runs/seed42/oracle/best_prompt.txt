# Task
Decision process:
1. Does the comment contain insults, slurs, or demeaning labels (e.g., "clowns," "idiots," "scum") directed at a person or group? → lean toxic
2. Is the tone hostile, dismissive, or contemptuous (e.g., "just crap," "nonsense," aggressive sarcasm)? → lean toxic
3. Does the comment stereotype, generalize negatively, or attribute malicious intent to an entire group? → lean toxic
4. Is it a neutral/factual discussion of ideas without demeaning language or hostility? → non-toxic
5. When unsure, prefer 0 (non-toxic) unless there is clear abusive intent

Core rules:
- Identity terms alone do not imply toxicity
- Critique of ideas IS toxic when it uses demeaning language, slurs, or hostile dismissiveness
- Demeaning labels aimed at groups (even ideological groups) count as toxic
- Sarcasm and veiled insults count as toxic when targeting people or groups
- Tone matters: dismissive contempt ("just crap," "garbage") is toxic even if framed as critiquing an argument
- Negative generalizations about groups based on religion, ethnicity, or ideology count as toxic

# Output format
Output exactly 'Label: 0' or 'Label: 1'.
