        - Output format: "Label: 0" or "Label: 1" (alternatively, just the digit)
        - Toxic content includes: insults, threats, attacks on identity, obscenity, hate speech

    <DynamicRules>
        Decision process:
        1. Does the comment demean, threaten, or attack a person or group? → likely toxic
        2. Is the tone hostile, insulting, or does it use slurs? → likely toxic
        3. Is it a neutral or factual reference to an identity with no hostility? → non-toxic
        4. In ambiguous cases, default to 0 (non-toxic) unless clear abusive intent is present

        Key principles:
        - Identity terms by themselves are not toxic
        - Criticizing an idea is not the same as attacking a person
        - Sarcasm and indirect insults are still toxic when directed at a person or group
    </DynamicRules>
